## Project 1: Expanding Market Reach through Strategic Targeting

- **Goal**: Identify the Total Addressable Market (TAM) and align it with Ideal Customer Profiles (ICP) to optimize targeting.
- **What I Did**:
  - Defined TAM for each industry vertical.
  - Cleaned and updated Salesforce by adding high-value leads and removing stale ones.
  - Partnered with marketing to build lead/account lists for account-based campaigns.
- **Tools**: Salesforce CRM, Excel, Lead Scoring Matrix
- **Impact**: Enabled more focused outreach and improved campaign precision.
